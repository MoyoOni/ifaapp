# Localization & Cultural Adaptation Plan for Ìlú Àṣẹ

## Overview

This document outlines the comprehensive localization and cultural adaptation strategy for the Ìlú Àṣẹ platform, designed to ensure the platform remains respectful, authentic, and accessible to Yoruba speakers and practitioners of Ifá traditions worldwide.

## Current State Assessment

### Existing Features
- Basic bilingual support (English/Yoruba) with toggle functionality
- Translation dictionary in [language-context.tsx](file:///c:/Users/Test/ifa_app/frontend/src/shared/contexts/language-context.tsx)
- Daily Yoruba word feature with pronunciation and cultural context
- Yoruba diacritics support throughout the platform
- Cultural terminology preservation (Àṣẹ, Babaláwo, etc.)

### Language Support
- Primary languages: English and Yorùbá
- Yoruba orthography with diacritics (à, á, è, é, ì, í, ò, ó, ù, ú, ẹ, ọ)
- Cultural terminology preserved (avoiding inappropriate translations)

## Cultural Adaptation Requirements

### 1. Linguistic Accuracy
- Proper Yoruba orthography with diacritics
- Accurate translations that preserve meaning
- Cultural context for translated terms
- Respectful handling of sacred terminology

### 2. Cultural Sensitivity
- Preservation of sacred concepts and practices
- Appropriate terminology for spiritual roles
- Respectful handling of traditional knowledge
- Recognition of regional dialects and variations

### 3. Regional Adaptation
- Support for Yoruba dialects across regions (Nigeria, Benin, Togo, Brazil, Cuba, etc.)
- Cultural context for different Yoruba communities
- Timezone defaults for West African regions
- Currency adaptation for regional markets
