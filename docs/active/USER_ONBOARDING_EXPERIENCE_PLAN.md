# User Onboarding Experience Plan for Ìlú Àṣẹ

## Overview

This document outlines the comprehensive user onboarding experience for the Ìlú Àṣẹ platform, designed to ensure new users have a smooth, culturally respectful, and engaging introduction to the Ifá digital heritage platform.

## Current State Assessment

### Existing Features
- Multi-step onboarding flow with role-based customization
- Cultural heritage reconnection pathway
- Role-specific setup (Babalawo, Vendor, Client, Admin)
- Progressive profile completion with resume functionality
- Audio-narrated welcome slides
- Yoruba language support and diacritics helper
- Privacy-conscious data collection

### User Journey Mapping

#### Client Journey
1. Welcome slides introducing the platform
2. Intent capture (reasons for joining)
3. Preferences (language, timezone)
4. Heritage reconnection question
5. Cultural onboarding (if applicable)
6. Discover temples step
7. Final profile details (Yoruba name, location)
8. Avatar upload

#### Babalawo Journey
1. Welcome slides
2. Intent capture
3. Preferences
4. Verification requirements explanation
5. Username selection
6. Credential upload
7. Heritage reconnection
8. Cultural onboarding (if applicable)
9. Profile completion

#### Vendor Journey
1. Welcome slides
2. Intent capture
3. Preferences
4. Shop registration
5. Heritage reconnection
6. Cultural onboarding (if applicable)
7. Profile completion

## Cultural Considerations

### Respectful Interaction Design
- Honor traditional greeting customs
- Respect sacred aspects of Ifá practice
- Provide appropriate guidance for newcomers
- Celebrate cultural connections

### Language and Terminology
- Proper use of Yoruba orthography and diacritics
- Culturally appropriate terminology (avoiding "like", "follow")
- Respectful alternatives ("acknowledge", "honor")
- Multilingual support with cultural context

### Privacy and Sacred Knowledge
- Protect sensitive spiritual information
- Respect privacy during spiritual consultations
- Safeguard sacred knowledge appropriately
- Comply with cultural protocols

## Risk Mitigation

### Cultural Appropriateness Risks
- Review with cultural advisors
- Beta testing with community members
- Regular cultural sensitivity audits
- Feedback mechanisms for concerns

### Technical Risks
- Progressive enhancement approach
- Fallback mechanisms for older browsers
- Graceful degradation for limited networks
- Data backup and recovery procedures

This onboarding experience plan ensures that new users of the Ìlú Àṣẹ platform have a welcoming, educational, and culturally respectful introduction to the Ifá digital heritage platform.
