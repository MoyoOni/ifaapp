# Community Guidelines & Moderation Plan for Ìlú Àṣẹ

## Overview

This document outlines the comprehensive community guidelines and moderation strategy for the Ìlú Àṣẹ platform, designed to ensure a respectful, safe, and culturally appropriate environment for all users while preserving the sacred nature of Ifá traditions and Yoruba culture.

## Core Values

### 1. Cultural Respect
- Honor Yoruba traditions, customs, and sacred knowledge
- Respect the spiritual significance of Ifá practices
- Preserve the dignity and authenticity of cultural expressions

### 2. Community Safety
- Ensure all interactions are respectful and constructive
- Protect vulnerable community members from harm
- Prevent harassment, discrimination, and abuse

### 3. Sacred Knowledge Protection
- Safeguard sensitive spiritual information
- Respect privacy during spiritual consultations
- Prevent commercial exploitation of sacred knowledge

### 4. Inclusive Participation
- Welcome all sincere seekers regardless of background
- Foster an environment of mutual respect and learning
- Balance accessibility with cultural authenticity

## Community Guidelines

### 1. Respectful Communication
- Use appropriate language that honors cultural values
- Avoid inflammatory, offensive, or discriminatory remarks
- Listen with openness and suspend judgment
- Acknowledge cultural differences with grace

### 2. Sacred Content Handling
- Treat spiritual matters with reverence and respect
- Do not share confidential spiritual information without permission
- Understand that some knowledge has restrictions based on tradition
- Respect the authority of qualified practitioners

### 3. Commercial Activity Guidelines
- Do not spam or engage in inappropriate marketing
- Clearly identify commercial content when appropriate
- Authenticate products and services honestly
- Respect the marketplace covenant

### 4. Cultural Sensitivity
- Recognize the diversity within Yoruba traditions
- Avoid making sweeping generalizations about culture
- Respect different lineages and interpretations
- Acknowledge that cultural practices may vary regionally

## Moderation Framework

### 1. Reporting System
- Easy-to-use reporting mechanism for inappropriate content
- Clear categories for different types of violations
- Anonymous reporting options for sensitive situations
- Direct appeals process for moderation decisions

### 2. Moderation Team Structure
- **Elder Moderators**: Senior practitioners with deep cultural knowledge
- **Community Moderators**: Active community members with good standing
- **Admin Moderators**: Platform administrators for serious violations
- **AI-Assisted Detection**: Automated systems for preliminary content review

### 3. Violation Categories

#### Tier 1: Minor Infractions
- Inappropriate language
- Mild disrespect toward traditions
- Non-harassing but culturally insensitive comments
- **Action**: Warning, education, temporary restrictions

#### Tier 2: Moderate Violations
- Harassment or targeted hostility
- Sharing of unauthorized sacred knowledge
- Violation of privacy
- Commercial exploitation of spiritual content
- **Action**: Temporary suspension, content removal, permanent warning

#### Tier 3: Severe Violations
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Threats of violence
- Explicit sexual content in inappropriate contexts
- Fraudulent claims about spiritual authority
- **Action**: Immediate account suspension, permanent ban consideration

#### Tier 4: Critical Violations
- Criminal activity
- Child endangerment or abuse
- Extremist content
- Crisis signals indicating self-harm
- **Action**: Immediate account termination, legal authorities notified

### 4. Moderation Process
1. **Detection**: Content flagged by users or AI systems
2. **Initial Review**: Community moderators assess the report
3. **Elder Consultation**: Cultural and spiritual implications evaluated
4. **Decision**: Appropriate action determined based on violation tier
5. **Notification**: User informed of action and appeal process
6. **Appeal**: Opportunity for user to contest moderation decision
